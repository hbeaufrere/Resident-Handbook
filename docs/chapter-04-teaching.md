# 4. Teaching Responsibilities

The house officers are also teachers to clients, students and technical support. While on clinics you will be expected to teach the students, provide them opportunities for self-learning and hands on skill development and provide feedback on their performance.

**Entrustable Professional Activities (EPA) must** **be completed for each student during the rotation**, preferentially as you evaluate students for physical examinations, history, etc.

**At the end of each rotation, student evaluations must be completed by the last day of the rotation.** Provide as many specific examples and constructive comments for each student as possible. Faculty will summarize for each student based on individual evaluations.

Numerous opportunities for formal teaching will occur, including didactic lectures, laboratory teaching, continuing education, community outreach, client education materials, etc. If you are asked to give a presentation by a student club or an outside of UC Davis organization, **notify your primary mentor before accepting the invitation**. Remember to share your presentations (PowerPoint) with the service by uploading it to the service Box folder.

You will instruct in laboratories given to 1<sup>st</sup> through 3<sup>rd</sup> year students. Opportunities for didactic lectures include: 1) Topic rounds for clinical students, 2) Elective courses VET436, VET437, 3) Student Exotics Clubs (AEMC and WAAM): 1 hour per resident per year (depending on schedule), or 4) Presentations give to local clubs (e.g. Rabbit House Society, Northern California Herpetological Society, etc.).

Tentative schedule for resident teaching expectations:

1.  <u>1<sup>st</sup> year</u>: Be able to present <u>all</u> the common clinical topic rounds, update & present 1-2 lectures in VET437 (4<sup>th</sup> year DVM – Avian/Reptile course), +/- national conference, +/- student club rounds. Residents will be assigned VET400 and 437 labs and direct self-learning sessions.

2.  <u>2<sup>nd</sup> year</u>: Develop student clinical topic rounds presentation, update & present lecture in VET436 (3<sup>rd</sup> year DVM, Small Mammal course) +/- national conference presentation. Residents will be assigned VET400.

3.  <u>3<sup>rd</sup> year</u>: Repeat Reptile or Avian Nutrition OR present new topic in VET 437, House Officer Seminar, +/- UC Davis Wildlife and Exotic Animal Symposium Presentation, +/- national conference, +/- student club rounds. Residents will be assigned VET400 and 437 labs

Previous presentations for students, basic articles for students, and hand-out for clients are available in the service BOX folders and CANVAS course folders for DVM 454, VET 436, and VET 437.
