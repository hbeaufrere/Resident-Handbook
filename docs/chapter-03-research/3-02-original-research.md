# 3.2 Original Research

The goals of the resident’s research endeavors are to obtain experience:

1)  with experimental design with defined hypothesis, objectives, statistical analysis, and data interpretation.

2)  in grant preparation/submission and scientific writing.

3)  presenting research results at scientific conferences.

4)  producing manuscripts for peer review publication.

Each resident is expected to complete at least one primary research project originating from UC Davis during the residency program. It is a requirement of the residency to complete a research project and present your original data at the UCD House Officers Seminar Day no later than the spring of the final year of your residency. It is a requirement for ACZM credentialing and for successful completion of the UC Davis residency that your original research manuscript be submitted to and accepted by a peer-reviewed journal according to [ACZM publication requirements](https://www.aczm.org/content.aspx?page_id=22&club_id=366916&module_id=49019).

You are **required to write and submit a grant** for a project. Identify a project that interests you **as soon as you can and discuss with your primary mentor***.* The first opportunity comes from the Center for Companion Animal Health (CCAH) resident grants (these are internal grants, <https://ccah.vetmed.ucdavis.edu>) with a October/November deadline of **your first year***.* Additional funding can be pursued through other granting agencies (see Appendix V).

When planning a research project, take into consideration the restricted time blocks available to residents (i.e. 1-2 week blocks of time for data collection). The research project should be **submitted for publication by the end of the first year** and the expectation is for the resident to present the results at a national or international conference such as AAZV, ExoticsCon/Exotic365, ICARE, AAV, ARAV, AEMV, IAAAM, etc, in addition to the UCD House Officers Seminar Day. See Appendix V for abstract submission deadlines.
