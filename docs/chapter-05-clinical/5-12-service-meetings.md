# 5.12 Service Meetings

1.  The second Thursday morning of each month or every other month, a Service Meeting is held with faculty, residents, staff, and supervisors from 9 AM-10 AM. Please submit agenda items to the Chief of Service in advance of the meeting. Student/Resident/Faculty rounds will be 8-9 AM to accommodate the service meeting.

2.  Faculty meet on Thursdays from 9-10 AM to discuss service needs and concerns.

3.  Doctors meeting occur as needed, but typically 2-4 times a year. Submit agenda items to the Chief of Service in advance of the meeting.
