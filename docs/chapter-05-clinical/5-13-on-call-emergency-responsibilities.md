# 5.13 On Call Emergency Responsibilities

1.  **Internal number of the emergency reception is 530-752-4844 during out of hours, as opposed to 530-752-9909 during regular business hours. Emergency Fee is \$361.00 regardless of time of day (1512).**

2.  Emergency cases, including emergency referrals, are seen Monday-Friday from 8AM to 5 PM and 9AM to 5 PM on weekends and holidays. Emergency hours may be expanded. Please be empathetic with clients that may arrive with critical patients up to 30 minutes after the 5 PM cut-off time.

3.  Most emergency cases are seen by the Emergency Line during the week. During weeks without the emergency line, cases are absorbed by the other receiving lines. In rare cases, (e.g. a clinician is sick) the resident on professional development may be required to help receive daytime emergencies if not actively involved in research.

4.  **Only faculty can indicate that an emergency should not or cannot be seen by the service.**

5.  One student will be on call each night, weekends, or holiday.

    1.  On weekends, the student will be notified with emergency calls. The student will talk with the client, obtain a brief (\<5min) history and inform the client of the emergency fee. The student will inform the client that the fee does not include diagnostics, medications, or emergency therapy. Then the student will notify the resident about the potential emergency. All the emergency phone calls received by the student will be discussed with the resident and logged in VMACS message logs. One student should sign up to be the “late” student each weekday in cases of an emergency case that requires care later into the evening. There are two weekend on call shifts for students, 1-2 students per shift. Students receive all calls up to 5 PM. If a VMTH client calls and wants to speak to a clinician during that time, those calls will be charged a \$75.00 consultation fee if they do not choose to come in. If the VMTH client comes in on emergency then the \$75.00 consultation fee is applied to the emergency fee. The house officer should call the student on call to receive the emergency with you. This program was implemented to reduce the number of after-hours information calls.

Wildlife Emergencies: The house officer on call is required to come in when eagles and wild mammals are presented on emergency due to their potential to injure the public. Currently, all raptors are received at the CRC, quarantined and may be tested for HPAI depending on assessed risks prior to being brought to the hospital.

6.  The VMTH does **not accept bats, deer, bears, mountain lions, foxes, skunks, or raccoons for medical care or rehabilitation. Any potential rabies vectors should be handled with PPE and faculty should be consulted prior to handling.**

7.  Faculty are always available for the house officer for on-call consultation. The house officers are welcome to call at any time. Please text first during the day. No text overnight, please call directly (text may not be read while asleep). Call if no response to text after 15 minutes.

8.  **On call compensation.** You are entitled to be paid \$45 per emergency seen out-of-hours. Please follow the House Officer On-Call Fee Policy. Make sure the on-call fee is entered to the invoice before the patient is discharged from the hospital.
