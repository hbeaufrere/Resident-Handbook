# 5.15 Additional Responsibilities - AAH,CRC,MGZ

**Records must be completed by the end of the week of the same week for Raptor Center, Micke Grove Zoo, and AAH (by 8 AM Saturday of the same week), however other VMTH appointments are given 10 days after evaluation**.

During CRC/MGZ rounds, the house officers will specify what follow-up is required on each case. Follow-up should be completed by the end of the week by the current team whenever possible. Follow-up that is expected to take longer (biopsies, outside lab tests, etc.) should be transferred to the incoming house officer of that receiving line during rounds. If the current house officer is unexpectedly able to complete a task, an email should be sent by Sunday evening to the incoming team transferring the responsibility.

For non-CRC/MGZ cases, the house officers retains ALL follow-up communication even past their assigned weeks on this service. For example, test results that are received for a patient after the house officer’s week on receiving 1, remain the responsibility of that house officer to follow up. If results are still pending prior to vacations or off-site clinical weeks, the house officer must make the faculty and other house officers aware of pending test results through the case transfer list (Appendix I). This list must be shared by email prior to departure.

House officers retain all responsibilities for attendance to journal clubs, rounds and service meetings unless instructed otherwise by faculty.
