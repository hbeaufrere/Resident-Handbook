# 5.22 Planning ahead for post-program interviews

As you approach the end of your program, you may begin scheduling interviews in preparation for your next professional opportunity. We encourage you to plan these interviews during your allotted vacation or professional development time, ideally toward the end of your program. Please be aware that we may not be able to accommodate additional time off from clinical responsibilities specifically for interviews, so advance planning is essential.
