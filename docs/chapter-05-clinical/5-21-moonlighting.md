# 5.21 Moonlighting

Moonlighting is strongly discouraged, as it is not considered compatible with the intensity and time commitments of this training program. On-call responsibilities have been historically reduced to provide house officers with additional time to focus on tasks related to clinical duties, educational development, and scholarly activities. **Furthermore, in the event of illness, it is expected that other house officers will make reasonable efforts to provide coverage for affected emergency shifts.**
