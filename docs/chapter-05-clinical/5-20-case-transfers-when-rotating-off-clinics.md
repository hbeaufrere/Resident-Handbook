# 5.20 Case Transfers When Rotating Off Clinics

When a house officer is on vacation or on an off rotation it is vital that any cases that require follow-up be communicated to the team to ensure timely and accurate follow-up with clients. Before leaving, house officers must

- Complete medical records, especially those that have planned recheck evaluations

- Fill out a case transfer form and email to the service all cases that have outstanding diagnostics or cases where clients are expected to call or need care or advice during your time away

- Email Service Coordinator with your time away so that calls and emails will be diverted and wth any requests for rechecks that need to be scheduled.

House officers must follow up on their own outstanding diagnostics and client follow up questions while on Professional Development.

Raptor center cases: In the absence of rounds the week prior, or if the house officer that will be on CRC that week is not present, ongoing cases of importance should be on the transfer list. The active case list should also be updated to ensure the entire team understands what results are pending or what follow-up is necessary.

Zoo cases: In the absence of rounds the week prior, or if the house officer that will be on MGZ that week is not present, ongoing cases of importance should be on the transfer list. Results that are pending and that are not received BEFORE you go off clinics should be included so the incoming resident knows what to follow up on. The active case list should also be updated to ensure the entire team understands what results are pending or what follow-up is necessary.

All medical records for receiving line 1 cases must be finished prior to rotating off the line of receiving to ensure timely communications with the staff at the facility as well as the resident rotating on to primary that will need to follow-up on specific cases **(by 8 AM Saturday of the same week).**
