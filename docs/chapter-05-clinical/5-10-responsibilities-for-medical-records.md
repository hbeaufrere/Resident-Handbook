# 5.10 Responsibilities for Medical Records

1.  Maintaining accurate and current medical records is mandatory and all in patient records should be reviewed at least once daily. Students are required to have records from the previous day complete by 8AM.

2.  Prepare discharge instructions **<u>in advance</u>** of discharge and get medications ready to go home prior to discharge. If a case is being discharged Monday, the resident should start the discharge instructions and prepare for drugs to go home <u>on Friday.</u>

3.  **All records must be completed and finalized by the primary clinician. There is zero tolerance for records finalized by administration (admin-final, performed 10d after discharge).** Prioritize record completion as 1) Referral cases, 2) Client-owned cases, 3) Wildlife cases. Prioritize cases that other clinicians will be seeing on rechecks even when it is before your 10 day limit.

4.  **You are required to complete records for Client-owned/Aquatic Animal health/CRC/Zoo cases before the end of the week of responsibility (by 8 AM on Saturday morning of the same week)**. Daily faculty/house officer rounds require resident preparation and prompt delivery of all pertinent history, physical examination findings, diagnostic tests performed and results, and appropriate literature searches to discuss diagnostic and therapeutic options.
