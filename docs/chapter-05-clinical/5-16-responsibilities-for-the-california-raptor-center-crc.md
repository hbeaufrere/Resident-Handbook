# 5.16 Responsibilities for the California Raptor Center (CRC)

1.  CRC offsite evaluation of birds happens at least once weekly, usually Monday from 10:30-noon with the CRC operations supervisors Julie Cotton and Marcia Metzler

<!-- -->

1.  Take with you:

    1.  The CRC supply toolbox, writing utensil, stethoscope. Emergency drugs are kept separately in the service ER drug cabinet.

    2.  +/-Panoptic/direct ophthalmoscope/Slit lamp if needed

    3.  Any birds that are being discharged from the service to CRC go with you, plus medications

2.  In hand exams at CRC on birds that require recheck, bandage changes, etc.

3.  Bring back any birds that require radiographs, ophthalmology consults, etc. Call front reception prior to request them to open the visit on the schedule for each bird and have a technician or a student have a cage ready for the patients. Alternatively, you can contact Julie or Billy to drop off the birds to the service in the morning, so the technicians can perform radiographs

4.  Review/discuss ALL rehabilitation animals and ALL active case resident birds verbally and/or visually

    1.  Review medications for all animals, ensure doses and duration of prescriptions, ensure prescriptions are filled and that there is enough medication for full course

    2.  Record most recent weight taken by CRC staff

5.  Record keeping

    1.  Make a new visit for

        1.  Every patient that comes to the service for procedures or diagnostics (ex: recheck rads)

        2.  Anytime a diagnostic test or procedure is submitted and requires interpretation

        3.  Any new problem for that animal

    2.  Add an addendum on the most recent visit for all other recheck examinations or discussions

<!-- -->

1.  With primary technician, ensure that the CRC boxes are fully stocked

2.  Update CRC active case list

    1.  <https://crc-active-case-list.vercel.app/login> , put your initials, password: CRC_Activated

    2.  Remove from active list birds that have been released, transferred, or euthanized

    3.  Update plan/follow up for next team

    4.  Review necropsy results and follow up with pathology too if necropsy results are delayed

3.  CRC case discussion is performed as needed between the house officers and faculty.
