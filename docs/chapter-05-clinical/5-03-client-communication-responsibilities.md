# 5.3 Client Communication Responsibilities

1.  Maintain appropriate communications with **every client, including MGZ and CRC**. This may require that the client be called later in the evening or early in the morning dependent on the client’s schedule or that email is used. **It is a requirement of the VMTH that clients be updated about their pets 2 times per 24 hours while hospitalized,** **one of which should be from the resident and include an update of the estimate (released in client portal**).

2.  The communication needs of the case should determine whether the faculty, house officer, or student maintains communication with the client. It is the house officer’s responsibility to ensure that the student meets the communication needs or ask from help from faculty if needed.

3.  **All client communications must be documented in the communication log in VMACS.** Out-patient follow-up should occur with the client within 12 hours of receipt of appropriate case management information (i.e. diagnostic test results, therapeutic plan changes).

4.  Please discuss with the client any changes in plans, requests for diagnostics, estimate increases, and case complications.

5.  Reminders can be put into VMACs for future follow-up communications with clients. It is your responsibility to maintain accurate and consistent follow-up on your cases, regardless of which service you may be on. **For example, follow-up on cases seen through receiving 1 are still the responsibility of the resident even if they have rotated onto receiving 2 / emergency line or are on Professional Development (PD), BUT** **if on vacation, conference or externships, cases need to be transferred to another resident if follow up is needed in your absence (Appendix I). Please note this is different than CRC/MGZ cases, which are transferred between residents weekly (see below).**

6.  The service email address is used to email information or discharge instructions to clients or to have them send us additional information

    1.  It can be accessed through online Outlook with the following login <ceams@ucdavis.edu> and password: Ceams2good2025!

        1.  Our Service Supervisor can provide ability to “Open Another Mailbox” when logged into your personal email.

    2.  Clients may send messages back to this email address which will be checked once per business day by service technicians. When providing this email, clients should be advised that a response may take up to 3 business days.

    3.  Respond within 48 hours to emails addressed to you.

    4.  **When possible, please use the VMACS Client Portal for communication instead of the service email.** This communication is automatically logged, ensuring the information is captured. Note that the Client Portal does *not* currently support attachments. Clients should be advised that a response may take 3-5 business days. It currently takes 3 days for a message sent to a clinician to “escalate” to the faculty on the case, so there can be delays if you are on vacation or externship.

    5.  Clients should be advised to call (instead using email or Portal) with urgent needs (530 752 1393).

7.  Non-Micke Grove Zoo related calls about institutionally owned zoo animals go to the Zoo Resident (see Zoo phone list for contact information) **BUT** Micke Grove Zoo calls/emails go to the resident and faculty on Primary Line or ER Line for that week.

    1.  Use the group text messaging option with Julie at MGZ with all service clinicians for updates on the zoo cases. This helps to keep us all informed.

8.  Aquatic Animal Health related calls/emails go to the AAH faculty or fellow initially and might be assigned ultimately to resident on Primary or ER Line that week, except during after-hour time or weekend. Calls regarding ongoing AAH cases will be routed to the specific clinician in charge of the case.
