# 5.14 Patient Charges

1.  All patients are required to have a signed estimate and treatment authorization on file at initial evaluation, regardless of whether they are outpatient or inpatient. A client portal is available where the client can sign documents and give deposits electronically. Please use the VMACS estimate generator. Ensure that each estimate is released to the client. If the case involves an extensive work up, it is of benefit to write the estimate in advance. Always cushion your estimates by at least 10% of the estimate. Important things to remember when generating quotes include:

    1.  Day Case fee (1501).

    2.  Sedation/anesthesia fees (1267), with recovery fee (9975)

    3.  Laboratory test interpretation fees (9982)

    4.  Tube feeding charge, this includes all day feedings (1549).

    5.  There is an additional charge for special food, i.e. baby food, Oxbow critical care.

    6.  SAPC ICW charges for overnight monitoring.

    7.  Service ICU charges for critical and more intense cases.

2.  Contact the front desk to collect further deposits every time the estimate increases more than 10%.

3.  Healthy elective small mammal spays/neuters are subsidized by the VMTH once monthly. Only one procedure can be performed on the designated day. The typical price for a non-subsidized spay is \$2,000-\$2,400 including the office call, anesthesia, surgery, and ligasure usage.

4.  Use the Weekend Billing Sheet (with patient stickers) for each inpatient case over the weekend; technicians will enter Monday morning. If the patient is leaving during the weekend, fill out the green billing sheet or call the front reception to add charges; please also leave a billing sheet for the technicians so they can confirm charges were entered correctly. To receive the on-call compensation, all billing must be entered prior to patient discharge.

5.  The technicians for the service help to ensure that the service is adequately recovering costs.
