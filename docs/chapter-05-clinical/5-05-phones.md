# 5.5 Phones

1\. The numbers for rDVMs to call the VMTH are 530-384-8234 or 530-752-4050. The number for clients to call is 530-752-1393.

2\. The phones in the house officer/faculty office and the technician area are for outgoing calls to clients, referring veterinarians, etc. **<u>Please</u>** do not give out the telephone numbers in these areas to clients, referring vets, etc.

3\. Telephone-Line Blocking

1.  **All UC Davis land lines utilize line blocking feature.** **Use \*67 before the number to block your number on cell phones.**

2.  If line unblocking is required by the client’s phone, **have reception call the client and transfer the call to you.**

<!-- -->

4.  Client communication responsibilities during time off clinics:

    1.  When on vacation or external rotation write an off-clinic message automatic reply on your email.

    2.  Change the greetings on cell phone voicemails.

    3.  Send a reminder to the service Coordinator (Cristin Dietrich) at <cmdietrich@ucdavis.edu> and cc the service team at <ceamsympa@ucdavis.edu> regarding the time you will be gone.
