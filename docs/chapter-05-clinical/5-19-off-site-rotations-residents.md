# 5.19 Off-Site Rotations - Residents

Several external rotations (or resident externships) are scheduled during the 3 years of the residency program. The purpose of these externships is to provide experiential learning opportunities for Zoological Companion Animal residents to assist in preparation for the ACZM credentialing exam. Over the 3 years of the program, these include 8 weeks at the Sacramento Zoo, 4 weeks at the Oakland Zoo and 2 weeks at the Marine Mammal Center in Sausalito, CA. It is challenging to schedule these externships for 4 individual residents over 3 years so the scheduling is tightly planned. Please be aware that there will be cost of living expenses incurred for these external rotations and these are the resident’s responsibility to budget for those costs, including housing and travel. Whenever possible, the faculty will try to identify a donor or a funding source to assist in covering the costs of these rotations.
