# 5.1 Professional Relations

1.  It is imperative that all the members within the service be able to interact easily and freely with each other.

2.  A “team approach” is a key component to working with our services. All team members should be willing to perform routine tasks (cleaning, feeding, patient admission, emergency case admission, etc.) to help out others on the team as needed.

3.  It is expected that doctors, technicians, technician assistants, and students participate in cases in a collegial manner and with appropriate professional communication.

4.  Communication between team members is critical to ensure an organized and efficient clinical schedule.

5.  Your same professionalism is expected when working with every other service in the VMTH and during your external rotations. You are a representative of UC Davis while on these rotations, and your professionalism reflects on our entire program.
