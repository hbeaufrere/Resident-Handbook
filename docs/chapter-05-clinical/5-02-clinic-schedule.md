# 5.2 Clinic schedule

The clinic schedule is dynamic and involves three lines of receiving. When assigned to each line of receiving, the day to day schedule is different. A general schedule for when we are receiving normally with 1-2 faculty and 2-3 residents is noted below. CRC = California Raptor Center; MGZ = Micke Grove Zoo; AAH = Aquatic Animal Health; OA = Rabbit/Rodent Occlusal adjustment. See Appendix III that lists that species we see and those that we do not see.

| **Line of Receiving** | **Monday**                            | **Tuesday** | **Wednesday** | **Thursday** | **Friday**   |
|-----------------------|---------------------------------------|-------------|---------------|--------------|--------------|
| **Receiving 1**       | Appts                                 | Procedures  | MGZ           | Appts        | OA/rechecks  |
| **Receiving 2**       | CRC (offsite)                         | Appts (4-6) | OA/rechecks   | Procedures   | AAH or Appts |
| **Emergency Line**    | Emergency appointments and procedures |             |               |              |              |

During the occasional 1 faculty, 1-2 house officer week (conferences, holidays, teaching blocks), this schedule may be simplified with no field calls for CRC, AAH, or MGZ. CRC and MGZ emergencies are generally brought to the VMTH. An AAH visit may still occur if Dr. Soto or the AAH fellow are available.
