# 5.18 Responsibilities for the Micke Grove Zoo (MGZ)

1.  Week prior zoo rounds – understand any follow up that is required from prior Receiving line 1 team

2.  Tuesday:

    1.  Planning rounds with faculty, resident, and technician 9-10 on Tuesday mornings

### Goals of meeting are:

1.  Assess if the caseload would benefit from having a technician come for the visit or if faculty/resident team only is best use of our service personnel resources.

2.  Review clinical procedures, ensure all materials that need to be collected are brought and roles for procedures/order of procedures are reviewed.

<!-- -->

2.  Check in with Julie to see if anything additional needs to be brought (refills from pharmacy, supplies).

<!-- -->

3.  Ensure you have reviewed the MGZ Preventative Medicine Program document available in Micke Grove Zoo Shared Box folder about the patients you are seeing, particularly those receiving annual exams.

4.  Trip to the zoo once weekly

    1.  Take with you:

        1.  Printed list of patients/procedures for the day

        2.  Any additional items requested by zoo or needed to manage cases of the day.

        3.  Medication/Control Drug Lock box \#21 (picked up from pharmacy by a Doctor)

    2.  In hands exam/visual exam and/or discussions on all animals on the list

        1.  Ensure that zoo is updated on outstanding finalized test results (necropsies, previously submitted tests)

    3.  Record keeping

        1.  Make a new visit for every patient that has an in-hand exam or a diagnostic sample that is being submitted to the laboratory (blood work, fecal analysis)

        2.  Add an addendum on the most recent visit for all other visual exam and/or discussions. Ensure that your initials or name are placed on the entry.

        3.  Upload diagnostic images taken that are tracking lesions to aid in communication between clinicians

        4.  Log all controlled drugs drawn up from the Zoo box and ensure all volumes are communicated to technicians for charging purposes.

        5.  Ask technician to upload any radiographs to PACS upon returning home

        6.  **Records (VMACS) should be completed by Saturday 8am after visit**, with few exceptions allowed (GMA week).

5.  Friday morning MGZ rounds are 9-10 and are shared with CRC rounds. This meeting should be attended by as many faculty and residents are possible. The goals of this meeting are:

    1.  Review active cases and plans. 

    2.  Review procedures performed/outstanding diagnostics. 

    3.  Review quarantine ins and outs.

    4.  Update next team upcoming procedures/plans.

6.  Zoo communications

    1.  A group text shared between clinicians and Julie is the best way to communicate about patient concerns.

    2.  Julie should receive verbal communications about all diagnostics and plans that were formulated at Zoo rounds. The receiving line 1 resident or faculty should talk with Julie on Friday after zoo rounds, or earlier, if concerning results to diagnostics are discovered that require immediate action.

    <!-- -->

    18. Responsibilities while on ER line of receiving

House officers while on the ER line of receiving should be available and ready to receive cases, including emergency referrals and CRC cases from 8am to 5pm. During this time, house officers should be on grounds and when not actively engaged in their own case management, they should work to facilitate in patient care, student engagement and/or management of cases for the other lines of receiving including bringing patients to consults, helping to recover patients, or facilitating diagnostics. Nonclinical duties should not be planned for during ER receiving weeks and should only be performed with the permission of receiving faculty after ensuring that the other lines of receiving do not need help in managing their case load.
