# 5.4 Referring Veterinarian Communication Responsibilities

1.  Developing and maintaining relationships with RDVMs is an important component of our service success. The following actions can be split by primary clinician and faculty

    1.  **Familiarize yourself with VMACs automated communications with rDVMS here:** <https://ucdsvm.knowledgeowl.com/help/what-communications-are-sent-to-rdvms>

    2.  Contact the referring veterinarian **within 24 hours** of patient presentation via a phone call, a referral letter, or email.

    3.  For hospitalized cases, upon initial contact with the referring veterinarian, ask if they would like daily updates on the case or just an update when the case is released. The rDVM should also be asked how they prefer to receive communication (e.g. phone call, e-mail, fax, etc).

    4.  For all cases, the rDVM should receive a referral letter using the “Clinician's Note To Veterinarian” within 24 hours following discharge (sent automatically by VMACS). The purpose of this letter is to thank the rDVM for their referral, outline communications/ concerns/ assessments that are not well highlighted in the medical record, and provide recommendations for recheck / treatment and whether that is planned at RDVM or VMTH.

2.  Faculty and third year residents that are on clinical duty share responsibility for call backs to rDVMs requesting consults or in need for estimates. Referring veterinarian calls should be taken immediately whenever possible, and must be returned the day on which they are received.
