# 6. Professional Development

1.  The house officer is expected to use Professional Development time to complete UCD research projects, to prepare for ACZM boards and credentialing (residents), to prepare for student teaching requirements, and to perform other duties that are directly associated with fulfilling the requirements of the program. Additional manuscripts (such as review articles) and lecture preparation outside of what is required should not interfere with your primary responsibilities (research, grant writing, manuscript writing to fulfill credentialing and resident requirements, journal club preparation) and studying for board examination. **Any trip to conferences or workshops requires faculty clearance.**

2.  While on professional development, the house officer is expected

    1.  To attend all journal clubs and didactic rounds (Zoo Path, Aquatic Animal Health)

    2.  To attend MGZ/CRC rounds if following week will be on Primary Line

    3.  To follow up on all outstanding diagnostics for client owned patients and client follow ups.

3.  While on professional development, the house officer is excused from

    1.  Clinical rounds

    2.  Student rounds
