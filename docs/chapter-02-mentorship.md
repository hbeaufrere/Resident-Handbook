# 2. House Officer Mentorship

Each house officer is assigned a Primary Residency Mentor. This faculty member will coordinate the house officer activities with the house officer and CEAMS faculty to meet the requirements to successfully complete their program and, for residents, to credential for the American College of Zoological Medicine examination.

It is the resident’s responsibility to be familiar with the most current ACZM suggested reading list, credentialing and examination requirements (www.aczm.org), and to meet the deadlines for the credential application (March 31st of the last year of the residency program) and examination application, in order to sit for the exam at the end of the residency program.
