## About this handbook

This handbook was designed to facilitate your transition from intern or private
practice veterinarian to the UC Companion Exotic Animal Medicine and Surgery
Service (CEAMS), the Aquatic Animal Health Service (AAH), and the American
College of Zoological Medicine — Zoological Companion Animal compliant training
program.

It describes house officer mentorship, responsibilities, and the protocols and
procedures that apply to the Companion Exotic Animal Medicine and Surgery,
Aquatic Animal Health, and Zoo Medicine services.
